OzoIon CleanAir System™

Ozone odor breakdown + negative ion residue purification

Pet odors are not just smells. They often come from odor-causing molecules, airborne residues, dust, and pet dander released by litter, moisture, urine, and daily pet activity. OzoIon CleanAir System™ is designed to help care for pet air in two ways: ozone helps break down odor molecules, while negative ions help attract lingering airborne particles and residues.
